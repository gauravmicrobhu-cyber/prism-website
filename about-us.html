About PRISM

An independent institute studying security threats

PRISM — the Policy Research Institute for India's Security, Intelligence & Modern Threats — studies security across borders, across domains, and across the line between a nation's internal life and its place in the world.

Internal and external security are usually studied as separate fields, with technology treated as a footnote to both. PRISM treats them as connected. A dispute inside a country's own borders affects how it negotiates abroad. An information campaign is part of a state's broader strategic posture. A biotechnology breakthrough can affect national security long before it is classified or regulated. That's why PRISM organizes its work around three pillars — Internal Security, Geopolitics & National Security, and Technology & National Security — instead of treating each in isolation.

Each pillar centers on one ongoing research project rather than a single finished report. These are updated as work continues, with sources shown alongside the findings.

Our Approach
  • Primary sources first. Declassified cables, verified incident reports, and direct dataset access take priority over secondary commentary, wherever they exist.
  • Every claim is tagged by how it was verified. Full text read, substantial excerpt, cited but not yet read, or secondary account — the distinction is kept visible.
  • Silence is reported honestly. When a research pull finds nothing new, that's logged along with the reasoning, rather than left out.
  • Gaps are noted, not hidden. When a data feed is stale or a claim can't be corroborated, that status is shown directly on the page.
Who We Are

PRISM is a small, independently founded research effort focused on ongoing research projects rather than one-off reports. Work is published under the institute's name rather than individual bylines.

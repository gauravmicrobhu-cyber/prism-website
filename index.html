Our approach

Internal and external security are usually studied as separate fields, with technology treated as a footnote to both. PRISM treats them as connected: a dispute inside a country's own borders affects how it negotiates abroad, an information campaign is part of a state's broader strategy, and a biotechnology breakthrough can affect national security before it's ever classified or regulated.

Each pillar centers on one ongoing research project rather than a single finished report. These are updated as work continues, with sources shown alongside the findings.

Consulting

Advisory Work

The same evidentiary discipline behind PRISM's public research instruments is available as direct advisory work for organizations navigating security-adjacent decisions.

Governments, businesses, and institutions increasingly need to make decisions in areas where the public record is incomplete, contested, or moving faster than policy can track — information operations, dual-use technology risk, and disputes with deep archival histories among them. PRISM's advisory work applies the same primary-source-first, confidence-tagged methodology used in our public trackers to specific, client-defined questions.

Areas of Engagement
  • Internal security risk assessment. Drawing on archival and documentary research methods to inform positions on long-running internal disputes.
  • Information warfare & narrative risk. Applying the same attribution standards used in the Pakistan Information Warfare Tracker to organization-specific threat monitoring.
  • Dual-use technology & biosecurity advisory. Extending CONTAINMENT's governance-gap analysis to specific technologies, partnerships, or regulatory questions.
Status

PRISM's consulting practice is newly established — there is no public client list to publish yet, and we won't invent one. If you'd like to discuss an engagement, reach out via the Contact page.

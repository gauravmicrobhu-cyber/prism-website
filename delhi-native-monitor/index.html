Operator profile

Attribution of the accounts to one operator was verified by the PRISM researcher through direct review of the operator's own videos and posts, and is supported by the open-source signals shown. The location is the researcher's assessment from the operator's spoken accent and has not been independently confirmed. No personal name is published.

Method: public Instagram and Threads posts are read twice daily; posts are flagged against a published codebook (framing non-natives as occupiers or invaders, calls for expulsion, threats, slurs, targeting of named people or religious sites, promotion of ranked or armed formations). Flags are research judgements, not legal findings. Quoted material is reproduced for documentation only. Links between accounts describe observed public behaviour, not identity, except where marked as researcher-verified.

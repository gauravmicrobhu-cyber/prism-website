PRISM's programmatic focus follows its three pillars. Named, standalone programs — fellowships, recurring convenings, structured research tracks — are still being built out.

Internal Security
Archival & Primary-Source Research

Geopolitics & National Security
Information Warfare Monitoring
Scheduled research pulls to detect, verify, and log state-attributed disinformation campaigns as they happen, not after the fact.
In development
Technology & National Security
Dual-Use Technology Governance
Tracking the gap between what emerging technologies — AI, quantum computing, synthetic biology, drones — can do and what governance currently constrains them from doing.
